Privacy Policy

Hermes Personal Assistant Bot · Operated by Paradoxical Worlds · Effective 10 October 2026

1. Who operates this Bot

This Discord bot application (the “Bot”) is a personal, non-commercial project operated by Paradoxical Worlds (the “Operator”). The Bot runs on the Operator’s own computer using the open-source Hermes Agent software. This policy explains what information the Bot processes, why, where it goes, and the choices you have.

2. Information the Bot processes

When you interact with the Bot, it processes the content of the messages you direct at it, including direct messages and messages that mention or address it in channels it can access. Along with message content, the Bot receives ordinary Discord metadata that Discord provides to all bots, such as your Discord user ID and username, and the server and channel IDs where the interaction happens, so that it can respond in the right place and recognise returning users.

3. Memory

The Bot is designed to remember. Facts, preferences, and useful procedures from conversations may be saved as notes, skills, and searchable conversation history in files stored on the Operator’s own computer, so that the Bot can respond consistently and improve over time. This memory is stored locally under the Operator’s control. It is not uploaded to any advertising, analytics, or data-broker service.

4. How your information is used

Your messages and the Bot’s memory are used only to operate the Bot: to understand your requests, to generate responses, to carry out tasks you ask for, and to maintain continuity between conversations. Your information is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties except the service providers described in the next section, and only as needed for the Bot to function.

5. Where processing happens and service providers

In its current configuration, the Bot’s language model runs locally on the Operator’s own computer (using the Ollama software), and your message content is processed there rather than sent to a cloud AI service. Discord itself transmits and stores messages under Discord’s own Privacy Policy. If the Operator later changes the Bot’s configuration to use an external model or tool provider (for example, a cloud language model or a web search service), message content relevant to a request would be sent to that provider to fulfil the request, under that provider’s own privacy terms, and this policy will be updated to say so.

6. Retention and deletion

Conversation history and memory are kept on the Operator’s computer until the Operator deletes them or you ask for them to be deleted. You may ask the Operator at any time to delete the Bot’s stored history and memory relating to you, or to tell you what is stored, by contacting the Operator as described below. Some information may remain in Discord itself, under Discord’s own retention rules, which the Operator does not control.

7. Security

The Bot’s data lives on a personal computer protected by the Operator’s ordinary account and device security. The Operator takes reasonable care with access to that machine and with the Bot’s credentials, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure, and the Bot is offered without any guarantee of security.

8. Children

The Bot is not directed at children. Discord requires its users to be at least 13 years old (or the higher minimum age in their country), and the Bot is intended only for users who meet Discord’s own age requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

The Operator may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted at this address, with its effective date shown at the top.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to view or delete your stored information, can be sent to the Operator through the GitHub repository where these pages are hosted (using its Issues feature), or through a direct message to the Operator on Discord.
